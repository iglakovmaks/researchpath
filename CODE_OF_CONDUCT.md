# Code of Conduct

ResearchPath is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
community.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainer.
Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
